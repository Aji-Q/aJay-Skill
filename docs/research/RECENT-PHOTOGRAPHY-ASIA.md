# Recent real photography shortlist — Shanghai + Hong Kong

**Research date:** 2026-09-09
**Capture-date window:** 2024-09-09 through 2026-09-09 (inclusive)
**Use:** source candidates for the immersive global-finance investment-council UI. These are real, single-image photography pages selected for composition first; the next stage may download the original and make a small image-tool reconstruction.

## Selection rules

- A candidate must have its own Wikimedia Commons file page, not a search/tag page.
- The **capture date** below is taken from the page's `Date` field and cross-checked against the page's EXIF `Date and time of data generation`/GPS date when present. A file-history upload date is listed separately and is **not** treated as the capture date.
- Licenses are limited to CC0, CC BY 4.0, or CC BY-SA 4.0 (all permit adaptation; attribution/share-alike requirements are noted).
- The Shanghai and Hong Kong sets intentionally avoid four same-looking generated “city-room” images: Shanghai covers a wide night skyline, a compressed telephoto tower view, and a street intersection; Hong Kong covers a compressed night footbridge, a finance-district street, and one wide harbour view.
- The direct original URL points to `upload.wikimedia.org`; the Commons page remains the authoritative place for attribution and license status.

## Handoff picks (start here)

1. **Shanghai — Shanghai Skyline 2025.jpg**: compact CC0 original, broad Lujiazui-from-the-Bund night composition; strongest wide Shanghai base.
2. **Hong Kong — HK Central night / Old Bailey Street footbridge (10 Dec 2025)**: CC0, vertical compressed Central street/footbridge view; strongest non-waterfront Hong Kong base.

These two have clearly different visual grammar: Shanghai is a wide, horizontal, illuminated skyline; Hong Kong is a close, vertical, layered street perspective.

### Shanghai composition notes

- **A:** broad, horizontal, night skyline/river geometry; useful as the “Shanghai macro market / city-scale capital” visual. Keep the river and skyline as a low-contrast layer rather than a generic room backdrop.
- **B:** portrait high-rise/telephoto compression from a park; a very different shape and depth cue from A. The original is unusually large, so use a resized copy for the image tool while retaining the original URL for provenance.
- **C:** daylight street intersection with location metadata; supplies ground-level urban density and everyday movement without repeating the skyline panorama. Prefer if the UI needs a road/flow metaphor.

### Hong Kong composition notes

- **A:** tight vertical, stairs/footbridge, night lighting and layered street depth; visually distinct from any harbour panorama and best for a compressed, high-density Hong Kong feel.
- **B:** vertical Central street view tied to Chater/Des Voeux/Pedder financial geography; good for a “capital flows through the street grid” treatment. It is CC0, so it is the lowest-friction edit source.
- **C:** the only intentionally wide waterfront candidate; use as a context card or optional alternate, not together with another wide harbour panorama. The page grants adaptation under CC BY-SA 4.0 and requires share-alike for a derivative.

## Provenance and edit checklist

1. Download only the **direct original** URL in the table; retain the original filename and Commons page URL in the asset manifest.
2. For CC0 sources, attribution is not legally required, but keep photographer and page credit in the project provenance record.
3. For CC BY 4.0 / CC BY-SA 4.0 sources, include photographer, source-page URL, license URL, and “edited/reconstructed” notice. For CC BY-SA, release the derivative under the same or a compatible license.
4. Do not replace or overwrite the Commons source files. Save edited outputs under new filenames and preserve the untouched originals.
5. Keep the reconstruction subtle: color grade, crop, atmospheric/light treatment, and modest compositional extension are appropriate; do not present the result as an untouched documentary photograph.

## Excluded search hits

Recent Flickr pages were not promoted when the individual page said **All rights reserved**, **full copyright**, or **Some rights reserved** without an adaptation-friendly license. Upload dates alone were also excluded when the page did not expose a reliable capture date. This prevents treating a 2025 upload as proof that the photograph itself was made in 2025.
